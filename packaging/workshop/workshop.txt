version=1
title=Talky - LibertyTalk Live Events
description=Talky connects your stream to your Project Zomboid game. When LibertyTalk receives a follow, a sub, bits, a raid, a channel point redemption or a Super Chat, it calls the Talky bridge running on your PC, and the mod plays the matching workflow in your game: a zombie named after the new follower, a horde sized by the raid, the helicopter for 1000 bits, a storm, an item for a donation.
description=
description=[h2]What you need[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Project Zomboid Build 42, in solo or as the host (dedicated servers are not supported)
description=[*]Windows 10 or 11 with Node.js 18 or later
description=[*]The Talky bridge, from the GitHub releases: https://github.com/Tech-Liberty/talky-project-zomboid/releases
description=[*]cloudflared, which gives the bridge a public HTTPS address
description=[*]A LibertyTalk account with Twitch or YouTube connected, and the "Project Zomboid · Talky" widget
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=Project Zomboid mods cannot open network connections. The bridge receives the webhooks from LibertyTalk and writes jobs to Zomboid/Lua, and the mod reads them every second. Press F8 in game to open the Talky window: bridge status, webhooks, workflows, game events and settings.
description=This Workshop item contains the mod only. Download the bridge zip from GitHub, extract it to a folder you keep (for example Documents/Talky, not Zomboid/mods) and double-click start-bridge.cmd: on its first start, the bridge creates its configuration and copies its token to the clipboard. Do not copy the Talky folder of the zip to Zomboid/mods: the game would see two mods with the id Talky.
description=
description=[h2]Limits[/h2]
description=Solo or host only. The address of a quick Cloudflare tunnel changes at each launch: paste the new one in the widget settings on LibertyTalk. Version 0.3.0 was tested against a simulated game API; please report anything that does not work in a real game on GitHub.
description=

description=[h2]Fonctionnement[/h2]
description=Les mods de Project Zomboid ne peuvent pas ouvrir de connexion réseau. Le bridge reçoit les webhooks de LibertyTalk et écrit des jobs dans Zomboid/Lua, que le mod lit chaque seconde. Appuyez sur F8 en jeu pour ouvrir la fenêtre Talky : état du bridge, webhooks, workflows, événements du jeu et réglages.
description=Cet élément du Workshop contient uniquement le mod. Téléchargez le zip du bridge sur GitHub, décompressez-le dans un dossier que vous gardez (par exemple Documents/Talky, pas dans Zomboid/mods) et double-cliquez sur start-bridge.cmd : au premier lancement, le bridge crée sa configuration et copie son jeton dans le presse-papiers. Ne copiez pas le dossier Talky du zip dans Zomboid/mods : le jeu verrait deux mods avec l'id Talky.

description=[h2]Limites[/h2]
